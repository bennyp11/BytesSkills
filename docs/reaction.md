# Reaction

Status: draft. The core mechanism is defined; specific examples and presentation
choices remain to be selected.

A person performs a short reaction, **roughly half a second**, which repeats as a
loop. Changing words, phrases or numbers recontextualize the on-screen hook question
and the apparent meaning of that repeated reaction. Often the motion is nodding yes
or shaking the head no, but other reactions can work.

The progression comes from changing context. It does not require a newly performed
reaction at every step.

## Two independent timings

- **Reaction loop:** approximately 0.5 seconds, with natural repeat boundaries.
- **Context beats:** each text/number state stays visible long enough to read and
  understand. It can span several repetitions of the reaction.

Half a second is not a prescribed text-change interval or the overall video length.
The final export may or may not itself loop; that is a separate ending decision.

Plan each context beat using its displayed value, meaning for the hook question,
reference to the shared reaction loop and text hold timing. Numeric values need
not increase. Music, optional effects and animations remain presentation choices.
Review source audio separately so repeating the video does not introduce unintended
speech fragments or clicks.
