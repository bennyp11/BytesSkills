# AI sweater weather

Status: candidate concept with sample delivery, not a finished script.

**Format:** troll v0.1.0

**Treatment:** deadpan v0.1.0

**Engagement:** rage + comment

**Topic:** Fall and ChatGPT or Claude

**Audience:** AI power users; accessible everyday habits per the user's subsequent direction.

**Premise:** Treat warmth in writing as a clothing or temperature setting.

**Sample straight-faced delivery:** “I asked ChatGPT to make my texts warmer for fall. Now it keeps saying “hope you’re well,” so I think it’s still set to business casual.”

**Audience help request:** “How do I change it to a chunky knit?”

**Shared knowledge:** Having AI rewrite ordinary messages and recognizing email clichés.

**Internal truth/bit note:** Warm tone is a writing quality, not a wardrobe mode.

**Performance:** Deliver sincerely. Let the mismatch carry the joke; avoid a wink or an explanatory ending.

**Production note:** Brand behavior, dialogue and situations here are fictional comic setups, not documented product features or real events. No performance outcome is guaranteed.
