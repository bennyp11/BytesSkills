Status: candidate concept with sample delivery, not a finished script.

**Format:** troll v0.1.0

**Treatment:** deadpan v0.1.0

**Engagement:** rage + comment

**Topic:** Fall and ChatGPT or Claude

**Internal truth/bit note:** The narrator treats software subscriptions as romantic obligations.

**Performance:** Deliver sincerely. Let the mismatch carry the joke; avoid a wink or an explanatory ending.
