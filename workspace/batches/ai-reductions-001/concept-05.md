# Language is processed in tokens

Status: candidate concept, not a final hook or script.

**Opening direction:** AI reads your words by breaking them apart.

**Topic:** AI

**Audience:** General audience

**Selected format:** reductions v0.1.0

**Discovery strategy:** Apparent contradictions

**Angle:** Explain how many language models turn text into tokens that can be whole words, pieces of words or other character sequences.

**ELI5 route:** A sentence packed into differently sized building blocks rather than one block for each word.

**Audience takeaway:** Words on your screen and the units a language model processes are not the same thing.

**Qualification:** Tokenization varies by model and language. Tokens are not always word fragments, and tokenization alone does not explain every model error.

**Research starting point:** https://platform.openai.com/tokenizer

Before scripting, verify specific examples and any claims about current products.
