# More context is not always better context

Status: candidate concept, not a final hook or script.

**Opening direction:** Giving AI more information can make its answer worse.

**Topic:** AI

**Audience:** General audience

**Selected format:** reductions v0.1.0

**Discovery strategy:** Apparent contradictions

**Angle:** Explain how irrelevant details can distract a language model from the information needed to answer a question.

**ELI5 route:** Giving someone directions with useful turns mixed into a long list of unrelated landmarks.

**Audience takeaway:** Choose relevant context rather than assuming a longer prompt is always better.

**Qualification:** Relevant detail often helps. The cited research concerns particular models and tasks; demonstrate the effect before claiming it for a current product.

**Research starting point:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00093

Before scripting, verify specific examples and any claims about current products.
