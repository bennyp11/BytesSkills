# Cuffing season — revision 3

**Derived from:** ../v2/concept-02.md

**User revision request:** Make the viewer feel like the subject of the story.

**Sample delivery:**

> It’s cuffing season and you’re still talking to two different people. One knows your whole life. The other just gets your writing. You keep telling yourself you’ll choose, but now you’re paying for both. Does using Claude count as cheating on ChatGPT?

**Structure:** Address the viewer as “you”; put them in a recognizable situation before revealing the AI in the final audience question.

**Performance:** Straight face. No AI names in opening hook text, early visuals, or captions ahead of their spoken reveal. The question invites advice or reactions about the viewer's situation.

**Status:** Fictional comic candidate. Format troll; tactics rage + comment. These revisions change this batch, not the global format.
