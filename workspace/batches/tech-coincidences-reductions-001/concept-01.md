# A day without a wrong turn

Status: candidate development route, not a finished script.

**Seed:** We are slowly removing coincidences from our lives.

**Topic:** Tech

**Format:** reductions v0.1.0

**Audience:** General audience (carried forward and reconfirmed in intake).

**Creative direction:** Accumulate recognizable examples into a realization, wonder and “wow, that is weird”; not an alarmist warning or ragebait.

**Angle:** Follow one ordinary day and notice how many small uncertainties can be resolved before they happen.

**Example progression:**

1. Check the weather before getting dressed.
2. Follow the fastest route rather than wandering down a different street.
3. Choose a cafe from reviews before reaching the neighborhood.
4. Pick the evening film from a recommendation instead of stumbling onto something already playing.

**Realization:** Each choice makes sense alone. Together, they can remove some of the detours where an unexpected experience might have happened.

**Possible landing before the format's follow CTA:** How much of your life began with something that was not the best option?

**Qualification:** Chance still exists, and useful planning can create room for other adventures. The point is the cumulative effect of smoothing away small uncertainties.

**Claim handling:** Treat the seed as an evocative premise about opportunities for serendipity, not a literal established trend that technology eliminates coincidences. Examples are illustrative possibilities, not measured prevalence claims. Verify any empirical claims or product-specific behavior added during scripting.
