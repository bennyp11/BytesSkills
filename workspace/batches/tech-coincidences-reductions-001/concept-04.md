# We are outsourcing the surprise

Status: candidate development route, not a finished script.

**Seed:** We are slowly removing coincidences from our lives.

**Topic:** Tech

**Format:** reductions v0.1.0

**Audience:** General audience (carried forward and reconfirmed in intake).

**Creative direction:** Accumulate recognizable examples into a realization, wonder and “wow, that is weird”; not an alarmist warning or ragebait.

**Angle:** Question whether coincidence disappears or whether more of its setup is now done by a system.

**Example progression:**

1. A song appears that feels uncannily right for the moment.
2. An unfamiliar creator arrives in your feed and becomes a favorite.
3. An app introduces two people who would not otherwise have crossed paths.
4. The experience still feels surprising even when there is a selection process behind the introduction.

**Realization:** Technology can create encounters as well as filter them. The shift may be from accidental proximity to selected introductions.

**Possible landing before the format's follow CTA:** The surprise is real. The way it found you has changed.

**Qualification:** No system plans the full outcome, and not every coincidence is algorithmically arranged. This route productively revises the opening rather than pretending the strongest literal claim is true.

**Claim handling:** Treat the seed as an evocative premise about opportunities for serendipity, not a literal established trend that technology eliminates coincidences. Examples are illustrative possibilities, not measured prevalence claims. Verify any empirical claims or product-specific behavior added during scripting.
