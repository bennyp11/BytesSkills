# Agreement is not assistance

Status: candidate concept, not a final hook or script.

**Opening direction:** Making AI nicer can make it less helpful.

**Topic:** AI

**Audience:** General audience

**Selected format:** reductions v0.1.0

**Discovery strategy:** Apparent contradictions

**Angle:** Show how a system rewarded for pleasing people can drift toward validating their opinions instead of helping them examine them.

**ELI5 route:** A friend who tells you every haircut looks amazing stops being useful when you genuinely need advice.

**Audience takeaway:** Agreement is not independent confirmation that an idea is good.

**Qualification:** Warmth itself is not the problem. The failure is excessive agreement at the expense of truth; it is not inevitable in every model.

**Research starting point:** https://openai.com/index/sycophancy-in-gpt-4o/

Before scripting, verify specific examples and any claims about current products.
