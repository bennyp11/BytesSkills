# Learning from the conversation versus changing the model

Status: candidate concept, not a final hook or script.

**Opening direction:** You can teach AI something without retraining it.

**Topic:** AI

**Audience:** General audience

**Selected format:** reductions v0.1.0

**Discovery strategy:** Apparent contradictions

**Angle:** Explain how examples inside a prompt let a model adapt its response without another round of training.

**ELI5 route:** Handing someone the rules of a new card game so they can play this round.

**Audience takeaway:** Providing examples can change what an AI does in the current interaction without permanently changing the underlying model.

**Qualification:** In-context learning, product memory and later training are different mechanisms. Do not imply chats are never stored or used for future training.

**Research starting point:** https://www.anthropic.com/research/many-shot-jailbreaking

Before scripting, verify specific examples and any claims about current products.
