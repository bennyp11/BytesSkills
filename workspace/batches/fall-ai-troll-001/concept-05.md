# Manifesting the weather

Status: candidate concept with sample delivery, not a finished script.

**Format:** troll v0.1.0

**Treatment:** deadpan v0.1.0

**Engagement:** rage + comment

**Topic:** Fall and ChatGPT or Claude

**Audience:** AI power users; accessible everyday habits per the user's subsequent direction.

**Premise:** Treat AI-generated planning and imagery as control over the weather.

**Sample straight-faced delivery:** “ChatGPT helped me make a fall vision board, but it’s still hot outside. I uploaded pictures of sweaters three times.”

**Audience help request:** “Am I supposed to hit regenerate, or is this a paid feature?”

**Shared knowledge:** Fall arriving on the calendar before the weather changes, and asking AI to plan your life.

**Internal truth/bit note:** An assistant or generated vision board cannot change outdoor weather.

**Performance:** Deliver sincerely. Let the mismatch carry the joke; avoid a wink or an explanatory ending.

**Production note:** Brand behavior, dialogue and situations here are fictional comic setups, not documented product features or real events. No performance outcome is guaranteed.
